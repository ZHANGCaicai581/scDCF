# Data sources: GWAS and Single-Cell RNA-seq datasets

This folder documents the datasets used in the scDCF analyses. SLE data will be added by the author subsequently; SJS and CKD datasets are publicly accessible as linked below.

## SLE (Systemic Lupus Erythematosus)

- Peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) from 41 disease samples and 18 controls
  - 33 childhood SLE (cSLE) patients, 11 healthy age-matched donors (cHD)
  - 8 adult SLE (aSLE) patients, 6 matched adult controls [18]
- Status: to be uploaded by the author.

## SJS (Sjögren’s Syndrome)

- Preprocessed dataset from Zenodo, salivary gland scRNA-seq
- 11 SJS patients and 5 control individuals [19]
- Source: https://zenodo.org/records/10884425
- Note: Use the original study’s cell type annotations as provided.

## CKD (Chronic Kidney Disease)

- Preprocessed dataset from CellxGene
- 28 CKD and 31 healthy control samples [20]
- Source: https://cellxgene.cziscience.com/datasets
- Note: Retain provided annotations without further modification.
